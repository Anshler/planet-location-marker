# Third-party notices

from this site; nothing is loaded from a CDN at run time. Exact source URLs, versions and SHA-256
hashes of every vendored file are recorded in [`vendor/VENDOR.json`](vendor/VENDOR.json)
(regenerate or verify with `node scripts/fetch-vendor.mjs [--check]`). Textures re-encoded from
those files are recorded, with their source, recipe and hash, in
[`vendor/DERIVED.json`](vendor/DERIVED.json) (`node scripts/build-textures.mjs [--check]`).

## GeoNames — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0

`data/cities.json` contains data from **GeoNames** (<https://www.geonames.org/>), licensed
under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Sources: `cities15000.zip`,
`countryInfo.txt`, `admin1CodesASCII.txt` and `timeZones.txt` from
<https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/>.

Changes made by `scripts/build-cities.mjs`: a subset of about 1,600 places (capitals, cities of
500,000 or more, the three largest per country and the largest per time zone); coordinates
rounded to 4 decimals; first-level admin and country names joined in; and the time zone of
places that GeoNames assigns to another country's zone (for example northern Vietnam →
`Asia/Bangkok`) replaced by their own country's single, identical zone (`Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`).

## Earth textures by Solar System Scope — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0

`earth_day_4096.jpg`, `earth_night_4096.jpg` and `earth_bump_roughness_clouds_4096.jpg` are
the textures used by the three.js `webgpu_tsl_earth` example, which credits them as "Earth
textures from Solar System Scope (resized and merged)". Solar System Scope distributes its
textures under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); they are based on
NASA imagery. © Solar System Scope, <https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/>.

Changes made by `scripts/build-textures.mjs` (the derived files keep the same licence and
attribution): `earth_day_2048.webp` and `earth_night_2048.webp` are the day and night maps
reduced to 2048×1024; `earth_clouds_2048.webp` and `earth_roughness_2048.webp` are the blue
(clouds) and green (roughness) channels of `earth_bump_roughness_clouds_4096.jpg`, reduced to
2048×1024 as greyscale and clamped to the value range the app uses; all four are re-encoded as
WebP.

The Max quality tier uses Solar System Scope's own 8192×4096 maps, downloaded from
<https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/> and pinned by SHA-256 in `vendor/VENDOR.json`, under
the same [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence and attribution
(© Solar System Scope; based on NASA imagery). `earth_day_8192.jpg` (`8k_earth_daymap.jpg`) and
`earth_night_8192.jpg` (`8k_earth_nightmap.jpg`) ship unchanged. Changes made by
`scripts/build-textures.mjs`: `earth_clouds_8192.webp` is `8k_earth_clouds.jpg` as greyscale,
clamped to the value range the app uses and re-encoded as WebP; `moon_4096.webp` is `8k_moon.jpg`
reduced to 4096×2048 and re-encoded as WebP.
